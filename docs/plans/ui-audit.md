Shot: grid-light-1440.png, grid-eye-390.png. File: `src/features/grid/ui/MixBar.tsx:57` (`text-on-dark` on the fuel colours). Owner: Abdu. Fix: darken the fuel fills or use dark text on orange and green.
2. **Skill cards on the phone map list: Locked cards, and the "Say it in 30 seconds" chip, are too faint.** Measured 2.0 to 2.8:1 for the card title, "Needs ..." line and chips in Light and Eye (Dark looked readable by eye, and the measured 1.5 there is probably the translucent card, so verify). Size: 390. Shot: map-eye-390.png, skill-eye-390.png. File: `src/features/skill-map/ui/SkillNodeView.tsx` (locked state, opacity or `text-ink-soft` on a tinted fill). Owner: Abdu.

Same inversion for the Sign in button, the Menu button and the "Map this topic" button. Maybe intentional, but it is the loudest thing on the page. Sizes: both. Shot: home-dark-1440.png, home-dark-390.png. File: `src/features/landing/PostingForm.tsx:345`, `src/app/globals.css` (the `--ink` token in dark). Owner: Abdu. 4. **Map nodes: "In the source" badge pokes past the card edge** on locked nodes (SQL queries, Time-series cleaning, Load forecast), by 2 to 5 px. Dark 1440 (also Light, Eye). Shot: map-dark-1440.png. File: `src/features/skill-map/ui/SkillNodeView.tsx` (badge row needs `flex-wrap` or a fixed node width). Owner: Abdu.
